Oasis privacy

Oasis is the task app of Oasis Life Church. It helps the church give out tasks, follow them up and keep the work in one place. This page says what Oasis keeps and why.

What Oasis keeps

  • Your name, position, email address, WhatsApp number, team and photo, if you add one.
  • The tasks you are given, your answers to them, and any files or links you hand in.
  • A record of the emails and phone notifications Oasis has sent you.

Why

Only to organise church work and tell you about your tasks. Oasis does not sell your details, show adverts or share your details with anyone outside the services listed below.

Who can see it

  • Other signed-in members can see your name, position and photo.
  • Only church admins can see your email address and number.
  • Files on a task can be seen by church admins and the person the task is for.

Visitors and newcomers

When someone visits the church for the first time, an usher or admin may note their name so the church can welcome them. A phone number or email is only kept if the visitor agreed to be contacted. Only church admins, and the one person asked to follow them up, can see these details. If a visitor says they do not want to be contacted, their number and email are removed. To ask about or remove your details, email oasislifechurch12@gmail.com.

Safeguarding

For people who serve with children or vulnerable adults, the church keeps the level and dates of their DBS check and safeguarding training, never the certificate number. Safeguarding concerns reported in Oasis, and these records, can only be seen by the church's safeguarding leads.

Services Oasis uses

  • Supabase stores your details, in London.
  • Brevo sends Oasis emails.
  • Google Firebase sends notifications to the Oasis phone app.
  • Google Drive: task files are kept in the church's own Google Drive. Oasis can only see the files it put there itself, not anything else in the Drive.
  • Google sign-in, if you use it, tells Oasis only your email address, so it knows who you are.

Your choices

You can change your details in Oasis at any time, under Me. You can delete your account there too, which removes your details from Oasis. To ask about your details, email oasislifechurch12@gmail.com.
